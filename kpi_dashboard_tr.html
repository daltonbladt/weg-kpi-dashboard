Definitions
How each indicator is calculated
Total stock value
Sum of Vl.Etq.T.D across all materials in the SAP report, on the reference date.
No movement > 180 days
Materials whose NºDias Con (days without consumption) is greater than 180. This is the SAP column used by the company's standard slow-moving calculation.
EOM projection
Same rule applied 16 days ahead — i.e. what the situation will look like on the 1st of next month, assuming no further issues. Useful as an early warning for next month's provision.
Provision loss
Per-material write-down weighted by aging band, summed across all materials.
181–360d → 25% 361–720d → 50% > 720d → 100%
Monthly history
For each month, the latest SAP file of that month is taken as the month-end snapshot. Cached in history_cache.json for incremental updates.
